# Featured Case Studies

## Access Control & Cost Optimization for Zoho Books

### Problem

The organization needed to provide financial visibility to multiple team members, but expanding user access inside Zoho Books increased licensing costs and created permission management challenges.

### Solution

Built a custom internal web platform integrated with Zoho Books APIs that acts as a controlled access layer. Employees can securely access only the required financial information without requiring additional direct Zoho Books user licenses.

### What I Built

* Secure API integration with Zoho Books
* Role-based access control
* Centralized dashboard for finance operations
* Controlled data exposure based on user permissions

### Outcome

Reduced dependency on additional Zoho Books seats while improving accessibility and operational efficiency.

## HRMS Platform for Attendance, Payroll & Employee Operations

### Problem

Managing employee attendance, payroll, check-in/check-out, leave tracking, and HR operations manually was time-consuming and expensive compared to subscription-based HR tools.

### Solution

Developed a complete HRMS platform to digitize employee operations and reduce recurring software dependency.

### What I Built

* Employee check-in / check-out system
* Attendance tracking
* Payroll generation
* Automated payslip generation and email delivery
* Leave management workflow
* Employee dashboard and HR admin panel

### Outcome

Simplified HR operations, reduced manual work, and centralized employee management into one platform.

## Interactive 3D Product Visualization Experience

### What I Built
